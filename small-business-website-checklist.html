--- layout: default title: A Small Business Website Checklist That Actually Matters ---

Most small business websites fail on the basics. Not on design. On the simple things a customer needs in the first ten seconds.

Here is the checklist we use at Codeair Software Solutions before we call any business website finished.

It loads fast on a phone. Most of your customers will visit from a phone, on mobile data. If your site takes more than a few seconds to open, half of them are already gone. Speed is not a technical detail. It is the difference between a visitor and a lost customer.

Your phone number is visible without scrolling. A customer who has to hunt for a way to contact you will not hunt. They will call the next business. Put the number and a WhatsApp button where the eye lands first.

It says clearly what you do, and for whom. "We provide solutions" tells a visitor nothing. "We repair ACs in Raipur, same day service" tells them everything. Say the service, say the city, say the promise.

Your Google Business Profile is connected. Your website and your Google listing should agree: same name, same address, same phone number, same hours. When they disagree, Google trusts you less, and customers get confused.

The enquiry form works. Test it yourself once a month. Fill it in, press send, and check that the message actually reaches you. A broken form is worse than no form, because the customer thinks you ignored them.

It has real photos. Your shop, your team, your work. Stock photos of strangers in an office in another country tell a local customer nothing. Real photos build trust in seconds.

It answers the questions customers always ask. Price range, timings, delivery area, booking process. Every question your website answers is a phone call your staff does not have to take.

That is the whole list. Nothing on it is expensive. Most of it is an afternoon of honest work. But a website that passes this checklist brings in enquiries every week, while a website that fails it sits there looking nice and doing nothing.
